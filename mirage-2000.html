MIRAGE 2000

Known in the IAF as 'Vajra' (Thunderbolt), the Mirage 2000 has been the tip of the spear in high-altitude conflict, defining Indian air superiority since 1985.

TOP SPEED Mach 2.2
SERVICE CEILING 55,770 ft
COMBAT RANGE 1,550 km

Precision & Power

SOURCE: DASSAULT AVIATION / IAF OFFICIAL HISTORY / PIB ARCHIVES

The Mirage 2000 features a tailless delta wing configuration, optimizing it for high-speed intercepts and exceptional low-level maneuverability. Its SNECMA M53-P2 turbofan engine produces over 95 kN of thrust with afterburner, allowing rapid climbing capabilities crucial in mountainous theaters.

The Kargil Crucible

"The Mirage 2000s changed the course of the conflict. Operating at extreme altitudes where performance margins are razor-thin, they delivered precision-guided munitions with devastating effect."

During the 1999 Kargil War, the Mirage 2000 proved invaluable. Stripped of conventional constraints, IAF squadrons adapted the aircraft for high-altitude bombing missions in the Himalayas. Dropping Laser-Guided Bombs (LGBs) on fortified bunkers at Tiger Hill and Muntho Dhalo from altitudes exceeding 30,000 feet, the 'Vajra' demonstrated unparalleled accuracy. Its advanced navigation and targeting pods allowed for day and night operations in treacherous terrain, significantly hastening the end of the conflict and solidifying its legendary status within the Indian Air Force.
