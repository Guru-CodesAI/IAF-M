02 / EXERCISE ARCHIVE

Strategic Maneuvers:
Exercise Tarang Shakti

A deep dive into the IAF's largest multilateral air exercise, fostering global cooperation and operational synergy.

Mission Objectives

hub

Interoperability

Seamless integration of varied aerial platforms and communication protocols across participating nations, ensuring unified command and control in complex combat scenarios.

radar

Tactical Integration

Executing synchronized strike missions, defensive counter-air operations, and dynamic targeting exercises to refine joint tactical doctrines.

bolt

Force Multipliers

Leveraging AWACS, mid-air refueling capabilities, and electronic warfare suites to extend the operational reach and lethality of the combined air armada.

Primary Asset

Dassault Rafale & Su-30MKI

The tip of the spear during Exercise Tarang Shakti, demonstrating unparalleled agility and BVR (Beyond Visual Range) engagement capabilities in dense electronic environments.

Total Sorties
1,200+
Across 3 phases of intensive tactical engagements.
Key Takeaway
Enhanced Readiness
Validated new operational paradigms for rapid deployment and sustained high-tempo operations.
