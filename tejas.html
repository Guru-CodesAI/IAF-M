02 / ORIGINS

A Legacy of Indigenous Engineering

"The Tejas represents a defining moment in India's journey towards self-reliance in aerospace technology."

Developed by the Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA) in collaboration with Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), the Tejas Mk1A incorporates numerous improvements over its predecessor, including mid-air refueling, advanced beyond-visual-range (BVR) missile capabilities, and updated radar systems.
